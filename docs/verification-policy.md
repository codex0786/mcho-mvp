# Verification policy

## Evidence categories

- Reported claim: an assertion made by a source.
- Observation: a human or instrument observation with method and time.
- Analytical inference: a conclusion derived from one or more observations.
- Review decision: an accountable human disposition of a claim or candidate.

## Publication requirements

- Preserve original source references and retrieval metadata.
- Record contradictory evidence and unresolved uncertainty.
- Do not infer perpetrator attribution from location or timing alone.
- Do not infer damage from unavailable imagery or no-change outputs over incomplete coverage.
- Require designated human review for high-impact claims.
- Corrections create revisions and a public correction note where appropriate.
- Satellite candidates remain candidates until reviewed.

## Review API

The initial shared-key mechanism is a development-only control. Production must use identity-based roles, key rotation, rate limiting, and audit review.
